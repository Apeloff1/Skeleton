# Design Bible Standards (10× depth)

Every full game creation package includes a design bible outline. Minimum sections and acceptance criteria:

1. High concept & pillars — 3–5 non-negotiable pillars with examples.
2. World & lore — consistency rules, timeline, no contradictions with critical path.
3. Characters — arcs that intersect gameplay; no orphan NPCs on critical path.
4. Verbs & systems — every verb has feedback, failure state, and teaching moment.
5. Level/room standards — connectivity, gating, hotspot density, save points.
6. Puzzle philosophy — fairness checklist, hint ladder requirement, no soft-locks.
7. Narrative delivery — environmental first; dialogue supports rather than replaces.
8. Art direction — era palette, silhouette rules, lighting consistency.
9. Audio direction — motif list, SFX feedback map, dynamic range limits.
10. UI/UX — never obscures scene; era-appropriate input.
11. Technical constraints — target cores, data formats, serialization.
12. Asset rules — naming, provenance, license, catalog entry required.
13. Accessibility — readable text, remappable where hardware allows, no color-only clues.
14. QA priorities — critical path, soft-locks, era violations, save integrity.

A bible is not complete until each section has at least one measurable gate.
