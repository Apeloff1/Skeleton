# CS-300 Cutting-Edge Computer Science Ladder

Status: **planned / 0 of 300 signed complete**

This is a machine-enforced depth overlay above Frontier-96. It does **not** create `VOL-421+`; the canonical breadth remains frozen at `VOL-000..420`.

## Qualification law

A layer is eligible only after the canonical Frontier-96 ladder is qualified and its earlier CS-300 dependencies are signed complete. Implementation and independent verification are separate signatures. Security, privacy, authority, tenant isolation, state integrity, rollback, human control and reproducibility remain non-compensable where applicable.

The ladder deliberately spans foundational theory through production systems and frontier research. Research layers are quarantined from production claims until their own promotion evidence exists.
