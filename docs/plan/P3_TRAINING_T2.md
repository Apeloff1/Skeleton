# P3-T2 Native Training and Model Improvement

P3-T2 consumes the 197-volume deferred queue left by the sealed P3-T1 autonomous-engineering frontier.

It schedules **19 volumes** and preserves **178 explicit queued volumes**.

The frontier is intentionally ordered:

1. **P3T-DATA-01** — distributed transaction strategy, cache/content identity, ingestion, document intelligence, lineage, data quality, dataset registry and synthetic data.
2. **P3T-TRAIN-01** — training control, distributed execution, checkpointing, elastic recovery and training observability.
3. **P3T-EVAL-01** — immutable training evaluation gates and an independently calibrated verifier program.
4. **P3T-POST-01** — post-training experiments, reinforcement-learning environments and curriculum progression.

This phase is aimed at a provider-independent AI that can improve its own local model stack under durable controls. It does **not** authorize self-promotion of a trained candidate into production.

Canonical authorities:

- `machine/ai_p3_training_execution_map.json`
- `machine/ai_p3_training_task_backlog.json`
- `scripts/check_p3_training_execution_map.py`

The exact 197-volume source boundary must remain preserved. Scheduled and queued refs are disjoint and exhaustive. Each scheduled volume has one owner, and all inherited masterplan obligations remain byte-for-byte equivalent at the semantic field level.
