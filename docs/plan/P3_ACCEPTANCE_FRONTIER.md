# P3 Acceptance Frontier

P3 acceptance is bounded evidence, not a maturity shortcut.

The composed acceptance layer covers three masterplan volumes:

- VOL-105 Autonomous AI Worker: budget, delegated authority, checkpoints, human
  override, recovery, and interruption/stale/conflict/deadline/resource stress.
- VOL-106 Research System: claim-to-evidence coverage, contradiction handling,
  reproducibility, uncertainty, negative-result preservation, and independent
  review.
- VOL-107 SOTA Candidate: exact benchmark/task/population scope, credible
  baselines, contamination audit, independent replay, robustness/security,
  latency/cost characterization, and independent verification.

The SOTA gate emits only a `bounded_sota_candidate` qualification. It does not
claim general or external state of the art.

Exact-head evidence is required before P3-ACCEPTANCE-01 can become
`landed_unpromoted`. Completion checkboxes and implementation/verification
signatures remain separate authorities.
