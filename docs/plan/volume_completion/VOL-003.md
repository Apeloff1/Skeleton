### L00 — Mandate and acceptance

### L01 — Interfaces and compatibility
Use canonical contract `ContractDescriptor`; document versioned schemas, compatibility, and migration without establishing a parallel authority.
### L02 — Ownership and dependency edges
Implement at `skeleton/contracts`, preserve canonical mirrors, and re-check the dependency DAG before introducing any integration edge.
### L03 — State and durable custody
Classify writes as authoritative/derived/cache/ephemeral; require replay-safe identities, tenant isolation, versioned migrations, and recovery receipts or an explicit stateless justification.
### L04 — Execution lifecycle
Specify bounded admission, startup readiness, cancellation/deadlines, steady-state pressure, shutdown, and restart semantics at the declared execution boundary.

### L07 — Executable verification
Run `skeleton/testing/test_contract_serialization_edges.py` plus adversarial negative, concurrency, cancellation, and crash-replay cases; `planned:` never counts as execution evidence.
### L08 — Evaluation and comparative baselines

### L09 — Observability and provenance
Bind redacted errors, metrics, policy decisions, and durable outcomes to operation ID, source revision, and traceable test artifacts.
### L10 — Economics and load
Measure relevant memory, GPU/CPU, disk, queue, and network ceilings; prove bounded worst-case load, p95/p99 latency, and meaningful admission refusal.
### L11 — Packaging and rollback
Document installation, migration, backward compatibility, backup/restore, deployment provenance, safe disable, and rollback verification.
