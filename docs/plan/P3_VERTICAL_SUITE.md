  review/verification and rollback evidence.
- VS-003 Scientific Researcher: source/method scope, preregistered metrics,
  reproduction evidence and preserved negative outcomes.
- VS-004 Multi-Agent Engineering: role scopes, conflict-domain detection,
  evidence-bearing handoffs and no authority amplification.
- VS-005 Self-Improvement: isolated challenger, four-axis preregistered metrics,
  independent promotion, canary and rollback.

  reconciliation and duplicate-effect prevention.
- VS-007 Desktop Product: signed clean install, governed AI operation,
  update/migration interruption evidence and rollback state continuity.

These fixtures prove fail-closed vertical contracts. They do not self-promote
the six masterplan volumes or replace platform-specific end-to-end release
matrices.

Run the exact suite through `.github/workflows/p3-vertical-suite.yml`.
