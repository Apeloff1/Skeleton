# Original Dialogue & Social Choice — Advanced Playable Family 31

Date: 2026-10-12 (Europe/Oslo). Status: draft until exact-head CI and root integration.

## Genuine playable scenario

This milestone promotes the original Dialogue & Social Choice editor from an
authoring-only form into a real offline, original social world. A human/player
moves physically among plaza, market and archive; up to eight individually
simulated speakers occupy defined spaces and carry independently authored
topic, position, statement strength, social agenda and finite patience.

Players actually hear original first-hand sources, choose support/challenge
by paying finite influence, vary trust and reputation, recover bounded energy,
and investigate two distinct direct sources on the same topic at the archive.
Only real evidence and support earn the nonterminal social-consensus milestone;
optional retirement ends the campaign. The world can deliberately remain
playable and unresolved when options, resources or dialogue lines run out.

Configured speaker catalogs can scale to 500. Non-active voices are explicitly
unmodeled; none are pretended to be individually enacted or omniscient.

## Substantial option growth

Sixteen new typed operational controls supplement the original five editor
fields: active speaker budget, conversational patience, starting trust,
trust cap, trust threshold, finite influence, initial reputation, support and
challenge influence cost, distinct trust/reputation consequence magnitudes,
minimum support goal, bounded social recovery and turn budget.

The original choice_consequences, memory_turns, lines_per_scene, speakers and
text_delivery settings are now used in actual gameplay, rather than only
configuration metadata.

## Causal and adversarial integrity

- Scenes have strict source keys, bounded original spatial stations, unique
  speaker identities, safe original names, fixed agendas, authenticated topic
  sources and no external script evaluation.
- A player must physically approach each speaker before obtaining direct
  first-hand evidence. Remote interviews cannot create witness accounts.
- Duplicated first-hand statements cannot fabricate a second witness.
- Corroboration requires two distinct source speakers with the same actual
  topic, reached by traveling to the archive.
- Consequential support/challenge changes finite influence, trust, patience
  and reputation. An author disabling consequence choices gets fail-closed
  support/challenge actions instead of fake progress.
- Speaker memory is bounded and expires; original first-hand evidence retains
  its explicit immutable source even when social memory decays.
- Replay receipts capture the entire resultant world, speaker state, source
  references and progression. Forged consensus, trust, testimony or resources
  fail source reexecution.
- Source copyright, production release, console hardware and native Dialogue
  support are not claimed.
