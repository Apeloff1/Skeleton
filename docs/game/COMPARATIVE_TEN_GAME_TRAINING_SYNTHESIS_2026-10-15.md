# Comparison synthesis and ten-game training curriculum — 2026-10-15

This layer operates exclusively on **actual native comparative study outputs** and associated reviewer-authored observations. It does not pretend that image/audio hashes prove game quality, physics fidelity, story continuity or AI wisdom.

- **Pairwise telemetry:** objective measured early/middle/after brightness differences, PCM amplitude differences and exact frame/audio equality across selected game pairs. Every comparison refers to immutable in-game stage evidence.
- **Viewer/Dragon/Jeeves notes:** only locally reviewed and stage-grounded claims enter the proposed insight buckets; author, epistemic type, game identity, dimension and exact evidence receipts remain attached.
- **Builder parameters:** typed numeric proposals, reported together with conflicting values/units, stay unverified hypotheses; they are not inferred from compiled games and are not installed into the builder or promoted into model weights.
- **Ten-game training cohorts:** successful native observations are grouped into <=10-game candidate cohorts, each carrying an exact set of canonical offline trace receipts and a proposed set of learning tasks. No training begins without separately verified ROM rights, approved training owner, evidence checkpoints and held-out evaluation.
- **Dragon crawler handoff:** pending curriculum identities are added to the existing compare-intake JSON, but authentication, reviewer approval, independent source verification and Dragon Family custody remain external. Viewers may browse these candidates in the standalone comparison report.

Typical next stage: an authorized evaluator chooses one cohort, verifies corresponding ROM and emulator rights, rates observed movement/audio/response behavior from real screenshots and traces, checks the same mechanics on held-out homebrew, and only then sends a bounded parameter/weight promotion proposal to the pre-existing Jeeves training owner.

The comparative study remains 2–30 games, at most ten simultaneous native environments, with no downloader and no proprietary game-data copying. No external game execution or model training was completed by this code commit.
