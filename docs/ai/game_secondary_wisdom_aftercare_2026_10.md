# Dragon / Jeeves / Adversarial secondary wisdom and post-weight assurance (October 2026)

Branch: \`feat/game-competitive-threat-radar-20261011\`, stacked on original AI-game reviewer work in PR #3643 and dual-runtime source in #3642.

## Intent: learn twice, without promoting an unqualified weight

New modules provide **actual** model-aftercare for the bounded local 256-feature game-review classifier and a guarded second learning experiment. This is not a pretext to silently modify foundation-model parameters. Dragon and Jeeves cannot claim to have read, trained or remembered an article merely because a URL or article hash was encountered. The external right to *reference* a public game article does not imply a right to incorporate its expression or train on its text.

The operations fall into four custody zones, each with separate permissions:

1. **Dragon acquisition:** existing \`webcrawler.dragon_research_missions.plan_research\` creates consented, bounded public research discovery intents for scholarly papers, articles, gameplay evidence and domain knowledge. Metadata alone is not acquired knowledge. Host fetching, robots/terms checks, rights/attribution, peer independence and privacy remain independently enforced.
2. **Wiki / almanac digestion:** \`ReviewedKnowledgeStore\` admits full source-revision evidence, notes, dates and dependence-group information. \`RightsLedger\` and \`ClearedResearchPacket\` authorize *reference* on exact source hashes. \`distill_dragon_peer_wisdom\` conservatively builds machine-friendly conceptual lenses, contradictions and original experiment tasks, **without** copying protected prose, code, images, audio or video.
3. **Original-game learner:** a preexisting \`FlowPlan\` partitions truly independent original game worlds into TRAIN/validation/TEST. The real reviewer SGD is fitted using **TRAIN-only original gameplay**, with validation-only hyperparameter selection, frozen TEST qualification, champion comparison and monotonic \`PromotionStandard\`. Legal reference-only third-party articles are not in the gradient dataset.
4. **Post-weight scrutiny and second wisdom:** examine the *actual* fitted policy's parameter deltas, independently replay qualification, aggregate attack and calibration failures, request **new** reviewed sources, proofread three nonidentical reasoning cycles using nine separate reviewer signatures, and refit only if entirely **new, independent original-game worlds** are available. Every output remains shadow/evaluation-only until the existing six-reviewer/usage/idle acceptance controls explicitly authorize the candidate.

## The first glance: measured training and weight aftercare

\`game_post_weight_forensics.analyze_post_weight_change\` requires a real \`OptimizationResult\` and the same exact original-game \`FlowPlan\`, quality standard and champion digest used during training. It compares policy SHA with the frozen winner, validates the actual policy schema and that TEST was not used in hyperparameter selection, then **re-executes** \`evaluate_candidate\`. Any mismatched qualification digest is rejected as tampering.

Recorded quantitative evidence includes:

- Number of changed parameter coordinates out of 256; normalized L1, Euclidean L2 and maximum absolute displacement, signed bias and decision-threshold shifts, relative parameter drift against incumbent weight norm.
- Per-TRAIN, per-validation and per-TEST n, independent project counts, balanced accuracy, Brier probability error, and critical-hazard recall. If the champion was installed, gain/regression is explicitly measured; without one, **no pre/post gain is fabricated**.
- 100% of covered heldout attack-family aggregates **by hash**, with source counts, correctness and missed critical cases; no protected example IDs, raw scenarios, predicted labels or heldout projects leave the sealed evaluator.
- Every original \`PromotionEvaluation\` blocker and an \`ImprovementLedger\` with train-only remediation. Ordinary accuracy gains cannot override calibration, critical recall, per-game regressions, potential norm explosions or missing fresh usage/idle evidence.
- \`shadow_fit_qualified\` versus **\`actual_active_installation_verified=False\`**. Actual optimizer weight fitting is not a user-approved active deployment.

Parameter drift thresholds are additional **defensive** advisory stop conditions, not a fictitious calibrated general-law threshold. Large changes demand human inspection; they are not inherently evidence that a newly trained model is better.

## Dragon repeat research: post-update discrepancies become queries, not gradients

\`plan_post_weight_dragon_research\` prepares a real \`ResearchMission\` from **aggregated** failures: regression, test shortcut detection, sparse original game diversity, dangerous misses, uncertainty, calibration, cross-world generalization and rights/quality frontier. Each plan also reserves gameplay, graphics, sound and storyline topics. Extra research interests may refer to source-safe, bounded Wiki mechanics, but **never** hidden test cases, their user/source IDs, content, exact labels or exact answers.

The mission currently uses four metadata discovery sources through the existing Dragon planner. This code does **not** fetch a page or guarantee those discovery sources contain enough relevant primary game-design research. No metadata gets adopted as a second independent vote until the full source, licence and peer/dependence group have been reviewed into Wiki and re-cleared.

## Three comprehension and repeated research passes

\`game_secondary_wisdom_loop\` requires a second \`DragonWisdomAlmanac\` for the same original project. The second Wiki state must genuinely add at least two different source identities from at least two **new independent publisher-dependence groups**; preserve every earlier source; carry a changed knowledge root and clearance receipt; and retain every earlier legal reference-right binding.

For each reviewed mechanic, three **different** tasks are created:

| Phase | Deep knowledge transformation | Adversarial safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Decomposition | Separate claim from assumption, measured feature, original-game inspiration and contextual caveats | Compare exact source identity, paraphrase drift, licence and republication dependence |
| 2. Adversarial reversal | Actively seek false or conflicting interpretations and independent negative controls | Scrutinize causality, confidence, missing controls, group dependence and old failed concepts |
| 3. Second wisdom / transfer | Propose original-world mechanics to test across new TRAIN, new validation and new sealed TEST games | Detect hidden test leakage, replay identity reuse, inaccessible game features, post-training regressions and drift |

Each of Dragon, Jeeves and Adversarial Mirror must sign **one substantive proofreading record per phase**, for nine separate actor-phase attestations. Every signature binds exact newly approved Wiki research, every task in the phase, a hashed **actual textual proofreading artifact**, quality score and the following six explicit proofread checks: evidence trace, legal source scope, logical consistency, measurement limitations, originality and test leakage. Missing tasks, unreviewed conflicts, unresolved objections, insufficient review score, invalid or shared credentials and forged text digests block approval. These are *cryptographic receipts for submissions*; real actor inference still requires the actual runtime to submit the review from its own configured identity, not self-asserted role strings.

### Secondary actual weight experiment

If the first optimization has not independently qualified, return a **blocked review report** and do not train. If no fresh original game worlds exist, return a **research complete / new gameplay required** report. Neither circumstance is a success label.

When new independent game evidence exists, an optional typed second \`FlowPlan\` must have **zero overlap** with *every* first run TRAIN, validation and TEST project. This prevents rebranding protected data as 'new' gradient input. Then \`run_integrated_learning_cycle\` verifies the expanded Wiki almanac and two unrelated original TRAIN worlds per mechanic, performs the actual second bounded SGD optimization and tests the second candidate against the frozen **first fitted policy as a shadow reference**, with fresh validation and new sealed TEST data.

\`analyze_post_weight_change\` runs a second independent audit of the new candidate. On regression, the prior policy is not replaced, and remediation starts again with **new** source reviewed game evidence. On qualification, the candidate is **still** not deployed: it must follow the existing game model registry's independent shadow approval, usage/idle evaluations and eventual authorization sequence. The comparison against the first fitted shadow model must not be marketed as a competition against a deployed commercial model.

## Strict historical reporting and distillation

\`game_wisdom_report_archive.publish_secondary_wisdom_report\` stores one canonical JSON under \`training/post_weight_wisdom/<sha256>.json\`, without overwrite or hidden truncation. It includes original/current Wiki and training-plan roots, all nine signed reviewer SHA receipts, digestion stage tasks, actual before/after post-weight summaries where fitted, unfinished legal or quality blockers, and explicit markers showing whether any real SGD occurred. \`verify_secondary_wisdom_report\` rechecks the digest, source JSON canonicity, duplicate keys and fixed signature count and rejects modified archives. Local SHA integrity does not constitute third-party notarization or authenticate real model identities.

This is an almanac-level machine wisdom report, not foundation-model memory. A fully fledged foundation-model fine-tuning or adapter promotion requires a separate train-licensed corpus, no train/test contamination, defined architecture and rollback controls, statistical generalization evidence, correct provider/host access, user opt-in and verified execution of a distinct model promotion stage. No such unapproved training happens here.
