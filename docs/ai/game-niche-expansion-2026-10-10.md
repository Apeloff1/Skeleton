# Gaming niches: research, original prototypes and product evidence

Status: executable research planning; no acquired niche dataset or deployed watch service.
Date: 2026-10-10. This catalog supplies 84 overlapping niches across 21 families, 252 mechanic entries, 84 proposed measurements, 84 failure probes and 84 original design prompts. These are authored research agendas, not empirical findings, an exhaustive taxonomy, established market opportunities or clearance opinions.

## Three floors within every niche

1. **Foundation:** identify titles and editions; abstract mechanics; collect primary implementation material, criticism and observed play; document player context; authenticate source custody; maintain rights and patent-family records. Keep incompatible builds and populations separate.
2. **Original prototype library:** combine abstract design lessons with independently authored code, assets, narrative and rules. Test each niche mechanic and each interaction. Record rejected designs, negative results and the provenance of every retained dependency.
3. **Products:** use a reviewed prototype as one input to a fresh creative brief. Reassess story structure, characters, dialogue, art, audio, interface, assets and implementation. Evaluate the finished work and its distribution jurisdictions independently. Changing era, genre or names is not clearance.

The existing `successor_blueprints.plan_successor_pipeline` remains the handoff for prototype/product review. Neither a catalog row nor a hybrid plan grants rights, training permission, memory promotion or release approval.

A query is not a citation; results must pass the existing custody, rights and reviewer-assessment boundaries before use. “Peer review of reviewers” means independent scrutiny of their methods, disclosures and claims; it is not automatically academic peer review. Publisher reputation is a lead to investigate, not a permanent truth label.

Do not require every creator to have covered every niche. Record no relevant coverage when appropriate; never fabricate an AVGN or Nostalgia Critic episode. Film commentary is relevant only when the connection to game adaptation, narrative or cultural reception is documented. Satire, staged failures, edits and persona are part of the interpretation.

Reviewer assessment should examine topic-specific expertise, direct observation, factual corrections, commercial ties, review-copy and sponsorship disclosures, platform/build identification and reproducibility. Independent critics may disagree in good faith about quality. Preserve attributed judgments rather than averaging them into a factual verdict. Track critics who cite one another or repeat the same press release as dependent evidence.

Let's Plays support timecoded observations about a particular play session. They do not by themselves establish average player performance. Engagement data describes measured attention in a defined window; it does not establish game quality, market size or purchase intent. Normalize by exposure where available, record missing denominators and distinguish organic traffic, promotion, bots and controversy.

## Evidence packet per niche

Each research plan leaves six evidence lanes explicitly unacquired: primary implementation, empirical measurement, observed play, independent criticism, accessibility and rights. Every proposed result needs a precise claim, exact edition/build/platform, source URL and locator, content digest, capture date, author/publisher lineage, methods, sample, relevant conditions, uncertainty and limitations. Record counterevidence and unresolved disputes.

The existing empirical promotion gate additionally requires custodied citations and independent support. Merely increasing source count does not establish independence: two reports about the same experiment remain one underlying experiment. Source authority depends on the question: a patent register supports a legal-status observation, a benchmark supports performance under its tested conditions, and a critic supports an attributed aesthetic judgment.

Preregister meaningful comparisons and exclusions. Compare devices, skill groups and accessibility configurations where relevant. Preserve negative results, raw traces and reconstruction instructions. Avoid extrapolating a small convenience sample into universal player preferences. Do not assign invented conversion, retention, fun or market-size scores to empty dossiers.

## Cross-cutting niche dimensions

Genre alone is insufficient. Each dossier asks for title/edition/region/language; build and mods; platform and firmware; input/display/audio latency; player experience; accessibility; session duration and interruptions; solo/local/online context; difficulty and seed; monetization and time cost; capture method and date.

Additional future acquisition slices include educational and serious games; cozy play; immersive simulations; stealth; survival crafting; MMOs and persistent online worlds; card, board and tabletop adaptations; licensed sports; regional traditions; local-language communities; experimental controllers; browser and offline-first games. The initial catalog is deliberately finite. These slices require their own sourced profiles and should not be counted as implemented catalog coverage.

For historical, homebrew and discontinued-platform research, distinguish real hardware, emulation, modified firmware and recreated engines. Record compiler/SDK provenance, timing, memory, storage, controller and display constraints. A platform catalog entry is not proof that a niche is implemented, successfully compiled or tested on hardware.

For community niches, record rulesets, category definitions, seed validity, replay verification, moderation burden and authorship of user content. For online experiments, account for matchmaking, network conditions, team composition and dropout rather than attributing all outcomes to the mechanic.

## Original combinations

Hybrid plans accept two to four niches and produce every pairwise review, up to six pairs. Each pair asks about timing, progression, recovery, cognitive and control demands, and whether one mechanic becomes busywork for the other. Compatibility remains untested until a prototype supplies evidence.

Examples of original briefs, not generated products or clearance findings:

- Cartography + cooperative puzzle: two surveyors use complementary instruments to map a fictional drifting archipelago. Test equitable agency and recovery after communication loss.
- Deck construction + ecosystem simulation: players assemble restoration protocols for an explicitly fictional habitat. Test strategy diversity while disclosing simplifications in the ecology model.
- Audio navigation + archaeology: explore invented resonant architecture through multimodal clues. Test orientation across headphone setups and accessible alternatives.
- Logistics + interactive drama: schedule community repair work while handling competing fictional testimony. Test whether narrative choices remain meaningful under optimization pressure.
- Precision platforming + experimental time: maintain an original weather machine using reversible movement. Test consistent rewind state and accessible timing controls.

No similarity threshold or formulaic change pattern guarantees lawful use. Abstract ideas and protected expression require separate analysis, and other rights may apply. A dormant franchise, unlicensed release, studio closure or abandonware label is not a rights grant. A lapsed patent can involve reinstatement questions; evaluate the particular claims, family, territory and current record.
