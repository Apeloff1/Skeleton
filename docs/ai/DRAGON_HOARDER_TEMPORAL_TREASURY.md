# Dragon Hoarder — temporal treasury, natural evolution and safe revisit learning

## Delivery and authority

The Hoarder uses the existing owner-scoped crawler checkpoints as durable observations. It is not a new model, storage engine or autonomous network agent. It retains earlier versions already present in separate expeditions, reads exactly witnessed snapshots, reconstructs version families on demand, and exposes separate current and historic index partitions. The current index means latest observed among selected qualified expeditions, not remote truth. No previous text is overwritten by recapture.

Historical-use permission is an explicit terminal-session command, bound to an immutable snapshot digest, each eligible preserved-text digest and source policy version. Owner authorization, source rights, policy versions, grant expiry, cancellation and deletion remain authoritative. Historical rights last 1–30 days, do not renew network/ordinary model-context permissions, and do not endorse third-party content as verified. A held newest observation never makes an earlier authorized version current by substitution.

## Deep architecture inspirations (reviewed Nov 15, 2026)

The following designs inform original implementations, not copied code, external-engine integrations, or measured superiority:

- [Apache Nutch adaptive fetch schedule](https://nutch.apache.org/documentation/javadoc/api/org/apache/nutch/crawl/AdaptiveFetchSchedule.html): distinguish changed/unchanged fetch history and schedule independently of indexing; Dragon's original grid posterior treats changed intervals as interval-censored observations rather than assuming a known change time or one event per fetch.
- [Heritrix bean reference](https://heritrix.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bean-reference.html) and [duplication reduction](https://github.com/internetarchive/heritrix3/wiki/Duplication-Reduction-Processors): retain revisit evidence separately from duplicate payloads. Digest-only aliases never obtain an independent source grant. A newer ambiguous alias can hold an older current entry. Conditional revalidation has a discovery-coverage tradeoff; this implementation does not transmit retained validators or claim a 304 renews source consent.
- [StormCrawler status persistence](https://stormcrawler.apache.org/docs/latest/index.html): status-driven scheduling, separate next-fetch calculation and storage. Dragon's change forecast is isolated from proven capture outcomes, and chronology is never inferred from an unsuccessful request.
- [Scrapy AutoThrottle](https://docs.scrapy.org/en/master/topics/autothrottle.html): avoid interpreting fast errors as permission to accelerate. Existing canonical latency/backoff duties remain independent of advisory revisit scores.
- [Crawlee queue backend](https://crawlee.dev/js/api/next/types/interface/RequestQueueBackend): explicit unique request identity, pending/handled lifecycle and reclaim without accidental new admission. The treasury uses existing canonical snapshot/owner/version fences; a read-time suggestion cannot become a request reservation. Eligible due families are ranked per origin and interleaved to avoid concentrating the suggestion list on one host. No distributed frontier claim.
- [Crawl4AI fit Markdown](https://docs.crawl4ai.com/core/fit-markdown/): maintain purpose-specific projections rather than conflating retrieval and raw content. Dragon reparses only already-preserved text; separate BM25 document-frequency populations, content-defined ordered section fingerprints and ordered numeric drift support temporal retrieval. No raw HTML reparse or external LLM call.

## Algorithms and bounds

Exact fetched URL plus media and verified representation variant defines a family. Changing normalized bytes creates a linked version; repeated exact payload extends its observation interval without new revision credit; a return to an earlier digest is a rollback. Simultaneous incompatible observations do not establish a reliable current pointer or ordered transition. Duplicate alerts without representation receipts can hold current status rather than inventing a family match. Exact retained-text digest memoization reuses one parse per distinct payload per request, never a cross-owner cache.

Bounded parser v2 uses at most 2,048 lexical tokens and 32 content-defined section fingerprints, a token-bounded trigram sketch, and ordered signed/decimal/percentage numeric tokens. A comparison reports approximate overlap, lexical term-count change, changed-section count and numeric drift; it cannot assert semantic correctness. BM25 scores are computed within the requested current/historic corpus. Latest observation and indexed evidence capture time remain separate when a newer identical but unavailable copy cannot provide text. Archive-only permission never places content in the current plane.

Revisit model: 31 fixed rates on a logarithmic grid, prior centered on one change/week, elapsed-interval likelihood of observed change at least once versus no observed change. Ambiguous timestamps and sub-minute intervals are excluded from interval evidence. Posterior mixture prediction is inverted for a value-sensitive stale-risk target and bounded to a 1h–7d interval; publisher hints and ambiguous alias holds can postpone. Ranking is expected stale-risk value per estimated retained-text cost, then interleaved geographically by origin hostname. It is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability, remote timestamp proof or authorization to fetch. A due suggestion can prefill a fresh bounded expedition; the operator must confirm its capture rights and authorize execution.

An exact source passage endpoint reads at most 4,000 Unicode characters, returns source, checkpoint, retained-text and span hashes, and fences the final row version. UI reads 1,600-character pages, respects expiry and accounts, and renders inert text. It never issues model-context or training permissions.

## Owner-wide expansion and recovery

The durable derivative is a compressed posting manifest stored in the existing session row, bound to the exact checkpoint and archive grant. Versioned lexical fingerprints avoid storing another plaintext copy (they are still sensitive derived data). Revoke and source-policy changes invalidate the segment. Expiry maintenance removes the derived index only; original user-owned captures remain governed by capture storage and explicit erase. Corrupt manifests fall back to reparsing still-authorized text. Retrieval separates current and historic document-frequency populations.

The owner-wide catalogue reserves up to 256 candidate slots for selected family, current heads and historically diverse representatives. Date-cutoff queries evaluate only observations and alias evidence at or before the requested date and keep all returned references in the historic plane. They never authorize a revisit or claim present-day truth. Cursor identities bind the actual partition and query. The backend now rechecks the complete scoped catalogue after reading, as well as row versions and permission expiry.

Legacy compatibility verifies witnessed terminal bytes and current source-use policy independently, joins exact retained capture event/measurement identity, and grants history-only use through the Historical Relic Recovery UI. It does not rewrite code identities or allow old execution. Held or unwitnessed sources remain unavailable.

An explicitly authorized watch covers only one admitted public source, at most six cycles in seven days with at least six hours between cycles. Each cycle has a deterministic child expedition, four request credits, one document, and a finite eight-slice allowance. The original crawler controls DNS, robots, authorization, retries and physical capacity. Each request and checkpoint publication rechecks the exact live watch-pulse claim, not merely the parent epoch. Revoking a watch prevents new IO but cannot retract a sent request. New grants are not silently renewed. Automatic maintenance depends on the existing application's recovery scheduler being active; no real live scheduler/device test is asserted.

The current final-stage animation already has 60 levels and 72 author-created additional rituals. ## Transition-aware capacity and watch recovery continuation

In a capped family, explicit comparison revision IDs are resolved using the same chronological identity as the lineage, including inaccessible metadata. Admission reserves independently usable representatives of the requested pair, exact latest observation and newest transition endpoints before duplicate observations. An older revision does not acquire current authority; unavailable text still cannot be compared. The normal global candidate limit and query partition remain unchanged.

An expired claim cannot settle; reclaimed pulses resume the same child. On lost replies after archival publication, the existing operation receipt and currently valid archival membership are checked without reissuing an altered idempotent command or renewing a revoked grant. Manual archive-withdrawal receipts prevent automatic grant creation. This is still a separate-row authority check, not a distributed transaction; concurrent parent revocation cannot undo already committed child storage or transmitted network requests. Independent recovery, fixed-host live exercise and final exact-head gate evidence remain necessary.

## Persistent derived-index care and capped-query parity

The existing recovery pulse independently rebuilds expected postings for at most two distinct owners with active archive grants. Only the exact witnessed terminal checkpoint and current grant/policy intersection are admitted. Legacy records require a previously committed witnessed archive-only receipt. A healthy audit advances only a maintenance cursor; missing, damaged or outdated derivatives are rebuilt through canonical storage admission under owner, status, version, marker, grant expiry and database-clock fences. Corrupt checkpoints never acquire authority from a cache. Storage denial defers five minutes; healthy segments are checked every six hours. Policy changes invalidate the derivative and make repair due; deletion removes the marker. No network retrieval, grant extension, training or unwitnessed migration occurs.

Canonical JSON size is accounted incrementally; ordinary materialization compresses once, with bounded binary trials for oversized compressed segments. This is not optimal packing or a permanent archive. The cockpit validates and presents the capped-corpus query-reranking receipt instead of rejecting the second backend strategy. New policy-subtraction, empty-index, corruption, compression, repair, healthy audit, storage-denial and version-race regressions are included in existing test suites. Actual database-clock and scheduler operation remain independently unqualified.

### Crash-resumable maintenance lane

Repair reserves one due row for a finite 180-second lease before CPU work or storage admission. Ancillary Mongo/storage failures no longer prevent the other two maintenance lanes from running, and cancellation still propagates. The read-side expiry fence includes active archival grants used for health and materialized-cache statistics even when their text also has current-use permission. ## Cold-custody tier and explicit historical windows

An explicitly acknowledged, expired terminal expedition can retire into a separate 128-ID quota in the existing owner quota document. Intent precedes reservation, then exact row publication precedes active slot release. Recovery resumes the durable marker; a crash can count both slots but cannot free active capacity before retirement. The original checkpoint is kept in place, without additional source permissions or indefinite history-use grants. Erasure releases both IDs. Selected windows of up to 32 retired identifiers are history-only and cursor-bound; default retrieval prioritizes active sessions and discloses truncation. No independent backup or cross-document Mongo transaction is claimed.

The cockpit now exposes restricted cold-custody selection, explicit retirement acknowledgement, retry, archive-use renewal/revocation, and owner erasure. The client checks exact selected IDs and denies any current plane, current discovery credit, revisit suggestion or out-of-window indexed source. Existing active mission lists stop counting retired sessions; retained bytes remain in the original session. New source and adapter regressions cover quota ordering and window custody.

Qualification includes a real Python→TypeScript history-only window, malformed selected scope/content/credit rejections, quota receipt validation and explicit retirement of independently witnessed older-runtime records. Cold custody does not grant model context and never extends capture consent.

Retired rows no longer crowd the legacy recovery catalogue. The release cockpit exposes the treasury even before a fresh session exists. If any owner-wide corpus cap is reached, global Hoarder qualification remains held under the previously defined completeness rule; selected history-only windows are not a workaround.

### Archive scale tier and automatic inverted routing

The cold reservation ceiling increases from 128 to 1,024 (32 active unchanged), supporting up to 65,536 captured source records in cold custody at the existing per-expedition maximum of 64. This is a bounded configuration ceiling, not measured corpus capacity. A same-row owner-scoped Mongo multikey index holds up to 4,096 domain-separated lexical keys per historical segment. Grants build it automatically, the existing maintenance lane backfills and independently rebuilds it, and source-policy mutation, grant revocation, expiry, restore and erase invalidate it. A bounded 64-candidate/16-live-recheck search routes to exact original sources and returns citation pointers only. The old exhaustive scan remains limited to 128 interactive chambers; indexed lookup is the larger-tier path. Routing truncation and partial candidate selection mean no global corpus completeness or BM25 claim. This follows the separate-data/index design of Common Crawl (https://www.commoncrawl.org/get-started and https://www.commoncrawl.org/url-index), not its physical distributed WARC/Parquet scale.
