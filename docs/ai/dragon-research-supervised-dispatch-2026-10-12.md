# Dragon research supervisor — queue-aware, idle-only dispatcher
**Implemented:** 2026-10-12 UTC
**Scope:** Dragon PR #3605, 248 curated candidate sources and canonical policy-stage admission.

## Milestone outcome

The Dragon's atlas, durable source-operations ledger and signed nest/resource plane now have a single, deliberate dispatch path. `DragonResearchSupervisor.reserve_next` takes the eligible policy-approved durable queue **first**, enforces real foreground/idle/consent checks, then asks the global scheduler for one resource ticket, and finally binds that exact ticket to the cross-process lease journal. It never executes a network request.

Previously, planning over the full 248-source atlas was separate from the durable queue; a source ready beyond the first 100 ranking results could be omitted, and an irrelevant or not-scheduled source could receive a resource ticket. The new `eligible_source_ids` filter is applied *before* atlas ranking, kept for every worker chunk check and cleared on actual worker-stop acknowledgement. The original public ranking behavior remains unchanged when no filter is supplied.

## Exact authority boundaries

1. **Trusted local supervisor:** owner authentication, task-relevant signed-nest state, device telemetry (no stale or thermal-limited sample), at least five minutes idle, and explicit article consent.
2. **Durable SQLite inventory:** `ready_candidates` reads only policy-stage-1, nonblocked sources in `scheduled` or `retry_wait`, respects explicit `ready_at`, validates full owner-scoped journal custody, checks current policy receipts, global host cooldown, and excludes leased/quarantined owners or hosts.
3. **Signed Dragon runtime:** the existing global resource scheduler issues a real memory/CPU reservation, narrowly scoped to one eligible source. There is no local-only grant fallback.
4. **Lease fencing:** the existing `attach` creates the durable host- and source-bound lease in an immediate transaction. On an admission race or newly blocked policy, the freshly allocated but **not yet started** resource grant is explicitly stopped and released, with no crawler launch.
5. **Actual worker:** only a caller with both the ticket and the durable lease may pass the separately governed article worker the `operations=` binding. Every HTTP chunk rechecks lease, source permissions and live foreground/resource conditions. The ticket itself confers no rights, content-retention approval or memory promotion.
6. **Revisions/review:** the existing content-sha256 capture ledger, Glean Matrix and independently authenticated Wiki/HOAG remain the canonical evidence path. No automatic training or XP advancement is generated by a discovery or dispatch result.

## Regression coverage

Existing source metadata, capture lineage, Glean, and safe HTTP transport suites remain wired to the Dragon AI Verification workflow. These tests are **authored**; exact-head execution must complete before anyone can call them passing.

## Known remaining integration work

- Process-global resource reconciliation still depends on a trusted supervisor. SQLite fencing detects unresolved leases, but cannot attest that a missing OS process stopped.
- Per-source robots crawl-delay beyond five seconds still requires an operator-governed long-delay dispatcher and refreshed policy receipts.
- Video and source-change specialist workers require separate media-specific content rights and transport adapters.
- No live acquisitions or AI weight training occurred in this commit. The 248 sources remain catalogue leads until independently assessed and observed.
