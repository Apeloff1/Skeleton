## Interpretation and next controls

Hyperlinks on one list, multiple Github repositories pointing at the same
engine, mirrored videos and pages on a single host **must not** be counted as
independent factual corroboration. A large fraction of the raw leads point
to GitHub and YouTube. This is breadth of candidates, not verified breadth
of independent content or author rights.

Every discovered source must still pass DNS/SSRF controls, robots and site
terms, source legality, item-specific media rights, quality/corroboration,
reviewed evidence and explicit consent before use. Nothing in these seven
supplements authorizes an external fetch, source replication, copyrighted
asset reuse, commercial game cloning, weights updates or deployment.

The source importer, heading almanacs, mission index and cross-engine
research portfolio can turn valid discovered references into **policy-stage
metadata queues**. Their outputs remain advisory until independent policy
workers and wiki/HOAG verification accept them. On this branch, no source
was approved for training or model-weight promotion.
