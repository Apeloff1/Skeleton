# Dragon living research companion — adaptive expedition curriculum

## Scope and actual connection

Dragon already had a practice-journal event protocol and a canonical source acquisition runtime. The new companion derives a living research state from the **same canonical CrawlRun snapshot**, not fabricated practice events or an independent agent. The crawler workspace and release cockpit display the validated state. The owner chooses one of four additional immutable expedition temperaments at creation; the original FIFO, balanced and coverage modes remain available. The academy's canonical feed includes the same server-projected companion and flight trace.

## Growth, novelty and intrinsic learning

For each retained source in canonical acquisition-sequence order, compute bounded NFKC/casefold lexical tokens. The tokenizer uses overlapping bigrams for continuous scripts and a maximum of 2,048 lexemes; no source vocabulary is persisted in the derived novelty archive. A bottom-96 sample of hashed trigrams represents each capture. Novelty N is one minus the maximum bottom-k union similarity against previously retained signatures (approximate lexical novelty, not semantic uniqueness). Exact content duplicates do not receive a second reward.

Let R be fractional objective term overlap (0.5 when no objective is supplied). Let Q be lexical richness times normalized Shannon entropy, both bounded by one. Per-origin cumulative credited novelty is M. The source credit is:

    credit = N * R * Q / sqrt(1 + M)

A candidate qualifies only when the content is at least 80 characters, N >= 0.25 and expected credit >= 0.05. This suppresses short pages, repeat keywords, near mirrors and monotonous same-origin collection. It does not prove source quality or ownership. Untrusted evidence remains unreviewed; no model weights or training permissions change.

Growth is an integer from 0 to 1000 derived from 40% discovery credit, 38% weighted objective coverage and 22% habitat/origin diversity. The five forms have thresholds 0, 120, 300, 550, 800. Efficiency and operational resilience are descriptive meters only. Invalid requests, access denials, quarantine incidents, retries, duplicate captures and rest cannot mint growth. Source-use restrictions hide reference identities without converting historical observations into current permission.

Recent learning progress is measured on the last eight settled content observations per acquisition hostname; captures contribute useful credited novelty, failures and redundant acquisitions contribute zero. Compare the mean of the later and earlier halves (minimum four observations), truncate regressions at zero, then shrink by sample count / (sample count + four). A prior-smoothed host reward and bounded uncertainty bonus temper sparse observations. This is intrinsic learning progress in a deterministic, finite heuristic, not gradient training or a scientifically calibrated probability.

## Homeostatic adaptation

The pet observes remaining request, document and total-byte budget (including uncertain reservations). Energy is the minimum of their remaining fractions. No wall-clock hunger, loss streak, token purchase, notification obligation or artificial decay exists.

- Explore: useful novelty and positive learning progress.
- Synthesize: uncovered objective terms and useful progress.
- Diversify: genuinely novel admitted origins and uncovered terms.
- Conserve: remaining energy below 15%; no additional companion bonus.
- Recover: degraded measured outcomes; no extra exploration bonus.
- Rest: no ready work, expired consent, paused/interrupted/terminal session. No growth penalty.

The controller blends the existing marginal-yield priority with curriculum need by at most 12%. Influence anneals as the finite budget falls; guardian halves the influence. Selection applies only after the existing eligibility filter. Robots, owner/tenant scope, canonical DNS binding, source rights, host cooldowns, byte/request reservation, physical leases and consent remain hard constraints. The companion has no separate network or autonomous execution authority.

## Skill tree and evidence identity

Twelve achievements are organized in four constellations: discovery, scholar, ecology, stewardship. Each has fixed prerequisites, finite metrics and applicability. Achievements are derived directly from the source contribution sequence, not minted into a mutable token wallet or a second journal. Completion references at most eight exact source identities, not source body text. Distinct origins are not proof of independent witnesses. The dashboard displays the current policy-filtered subset of references. The digest identifies the underlying derived canonical questbook, not the filtered wire view.

Each new capture is linked one-to-one to its corresponding settled measurement's source ID, host and event sequence. Pending companion decisions record the strategy, quest, SHA-256 candidate fingerprint, count of eligible rows, base versus adjusted score, and policy/content phase **before** the canonical transport call. The settled measurement inherits the same decision. When checkpoint recovery cannot confirm a dispatch outcome, its reservation is retained and its receipt settles as an uncertain failure without another fetch or source credit. Captured decisions are checked against the actual fetched URL. A retained operational event must agree with its measurement's outcome, source ID and hostname. The trace exposes no new URL permission.

## Evaluation

The dedicated source tests exercise:
- exact duplicates and keyword padding;
- genuine novelty and format/origin coverage;
- inactivity, consent expiry and failed-acquisition non-reward;
- continuous-script bounded tokenization;
- low-energy/guardian exploration bounds and held frontier eligibility;
- actual pre-dispatch reservation and settlement identity, checkpoint restoration, forged source and decision identities;
- uncertain in-flight recovery without a second network call or fabricated growth.

The shared offline benchmark compares eight modes, four fixed corpora, and three configured repetitions (96 planned fixture runs). The added corpus mixes exact mirrored content, repetitive padding and distinct substantive sources. The transport latency quantum is controlled for reproducibility; the elapsed wall time is a fixture runtime observation, not live-web throughput. No external engine is executed. No competitive superiority or production readiness follows from source additions alone.

## Cross-expedition sanctuary

The owner may explicitly observe a current-consent collection via the authenticated sanctuary endpoint. It selects at most eight recent expeditions; each contributes at most sixteen fresh, currently enabled, unquarantined source rows. The backend verifies the exact witnessed primary checkpoint, owner scope, request/consent binding and source-use policy. It excludes invalid revisions, reports truncation, and rereads every selected version and consent boundary before issuing the result; revision races return a conflict. This is a bounded read with version fencing, not a cross-document transaction.

Cross-session copies are deduplicated by exact content hash; approximate lexical novelty and per-origin credit saturation reduce near-copy contribution. The collection is 55% saturating useful novelty credit, 25% origin diversity, and 20% format diversity. Its five-form index can change as source permissions or freshness change; it is not pet health, permanent XP, or a reward penalty. No raw text, query or URL appears in the response. The observation is explicitly refreshed and locally expires after a maximum of five minutes or the earliest selected source expiration. The UI invalidates its view on observed session revisions and account changes. Backend tests cover actual canonical captures, withdrawal, incompatible scope, and concurrent compare failure.

## Animated companion

The existing animated Dragon actor now receives actual evidence growth, five palettes and shell heights, completed-achievement cosmetic unlocks and canonical rest/quest posture. Its existing boop interaction is purely decorative. Reduced-motion, screen-reader and device power preferences suppress motion. The duplicate workspace actor is removed; both crawler workspace and release cockpit use the same companion panel. Cosmetic animation never represents a knowledge burn or training operation.

The sanctuary does not create permanent account-wide lifetime memory, model-weight personalization, or a cross-session scheduler. More than eight expeditions, cross-document atomicity, durable revocable lifelong achievements and native device validation are remaining work. Old snapshots bound to older implementation digests fail closed; migration needs its own reviewed contract. Exact-head CI, independent Windows/WSL2, security and live authorized-network tests remain mandatory before deployment.
