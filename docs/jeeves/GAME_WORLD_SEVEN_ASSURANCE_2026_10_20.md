# Seven integrated authored-game world assurance milestones

1. Original virtual-world XYZ geometry and topology diagnostics.
2. Directed forward/reverse reachability and dead-end identification.
3. Deterministic bounded edge-failure and goal isolation probes.
4. Individual capability-loss probes with actual gameplay route execution.
5. Full original ExpeditionEpisode destination-winning replay and receipts.
6. Canonical story, quest, choices, authored flags and ending references.
7. Second independent DFS/BFS comparison and adversarial receipt-tampering scrutiny.

The canonical Jeeves campaign compiler exports game/frontier/world_assurance.json, binds its SHA256 receipt in the source manifest, returns it to CLI, and independently recompiles it with all other files before export. No new provider or runtime service is created. Work is bounded to 4096 nodes, 16384 directed edges, 16 destinations, 32 individual capability probes and up to 192 default failure-edge probes.

Do not infer real GPS, engine-native gameplay, hardware qualification, legal rights, long-form endurance, model-weight learning, or production signoff from source audits. Existing architecture and release checks remain authoritative. The native Godot and Backend Quality workflows must qualify exact-head changes before merging.
